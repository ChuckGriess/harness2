# AI Ecosystem Watchlist
Lens: Tim O'Reilly. Track tools and voices that spread capability across the ecosystem instead of concentrating it.

## People

- **Tim O'Reilly**
  Focus: Economics and governance of AI, especially mechanism design for the human-AI economy and disclosure standards for how AI is commercialized
  Roles: Founder, CEO and chairman of O'Reilly Media; co-director of the AI Disclosures Project at the SSRC (with economist Ilan Strauss); partner at O'Reilly AlphaTech Ventures
  Lens fit: Origin of the lens ("create more value than you capture")
  Follow: O'Reilly Radar (Substack), Asimov's Addendum (asimovaddendum.substack.com), Conversations with AI (substack.com/@timoreilly), LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/timo3), oreilly.com/tim

- **Andrew Ng**
  Focus: AI education at scale, agentic AI workflows, applied AI adoption and venture building
  Roles: Founder of DeepLearning.AI; managing general partner at AI Fund; managing partner and co-founder of AI Aspire; executive chairman of LandingAI; chairman and co-founder of Coursera; adjunct professor at Stanford
  Lens fit: Teaches practical AI skills to a mass audience instead of keeping them inside a few labs
  Follow: The Batch (deeplearning.ai/the-batch), X (@AndrewYNg), LinkedIn

- **Cassie Kozyrkov**
  Focus: Decision intelligence and human workflows
  Lens fit: Human augmentation over cost cutting
  Follow: LinkedIn, personal blog

- **Ethan Mollick**
  Focus: Real-world workplace productivity with AI
  Lens fit: Value creation over hype
  Follow: One Useful Thing (Substack), X

- **Andrej Karpathy**

  Lens fit: Systems thinking and truthfulness
  Follow: X, YouTube, GitHub

- **Jeremy Howard**

## Organizations

- **O'Reilly Media**
  Focus: Technical learning platform (books, video, live training, hands-on labs, the AI-powered O'Reilly Answers)
  Events: AI Codecon, free online events on building software with AI, co-hosted by Tim O'Reilly and Addy Osmani
  Lens fit: Spreads practitioners' knowledge widely instead of keeping it inside a few companies
  Follow: oreilly.com/radar, AI Codecon event pages

- **DeepLearning.AI**
  Focus: AI education through full courses, specializations, and short courses (many built with AI labs and tool vendors, e.g. MCP with Anthropic)
  Lens fit: Spreads hands-on skills for building with AI across a very large audience
  Follow: The Batch (deeplearning.ai/the-batch), course catalog (deeplearning.ai/courses), community.deeplearning.ai

- **Thinking Machines Lab (Mira Murati)**
  Focus: Lowering the infrastructure barrier to post-training and fine-tuning
  Products:
    - Tinker: Python-native cloud API for fine-tuning and RL on open-weight models (1B to 1T+ params). It manages distributed GPU training for you.
    - Inkling: open-weight multimodal model family
  Lens fit: Publishes open weights on Hugging Face and charges only for infrastructure
  Follow: thinkingmachines.ai/news, github.com/thinking-machines-lab/tinker-cookbook, tinker-docs.thinkingmachines.ai

- **Hugging Face**
  Focus: Open-weight model ecosystem
  Lens fit: Decentralization, a counterweight to monopolies
  Follow: huggingface.co

- **Anthropic**
  Focus: Mechanistic interpretability and safety
  Lens fit: Structural alignment and governance
  Follow: Research papers, company roadmap posts

- **Databricks / Dataiku**
  Focus: Enterprise data infrastructure and pipelines
  Lens fit: Applied utility over chatbot hype
  Follow: Company tech blogs, enterprise case studies
